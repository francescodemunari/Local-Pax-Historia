Command Briefing

Your decisions are orders. The Game Master resolves their consequences.

  1. Read the map. Click a region to inspect its controller, related events, and formations. Use the map search or zoom controls to orient yourself.
  2. Issue an order. Open Actions and write a clear political, military, economic, or diplomatic instruction. Orders remain pending until time advances.
  3. Request formations. Ask to raise a division in a controlled region. You do not create it directly: the Game Master considers the order, resources, realism, and the scenario unit catalog.
  4. Advance time. Choose a time jump. The Game Master resolves pending orders, world events, map control, and any approved formations.
  5. Respond to the world. Review Events, consult the Advisor, and use Diplomacy to negotiate with other nations before committing to the next turn.
Tip: precise places and objectives produce better resolutions.
