Map sources

Geographic scenarios use dated boundaries and capitals from CShapes 2.0, by Schvitz, Rüegger, Girardin, Cederman, Weidmann and Gleditsch (2022). Data and adapted geographic outputs are licensed under CC BY-NC-SA 4.0, including the noncommercial restriction.

Province boundaries, city coordinate refinements and background land use public-domain Natural Earth data.

Adapted 14 September 2026: repaired and simplified geometry, clipped modern administrative provinces to dated country borders, projected city coordinates, and scenario control overlays. Historical internal divisions remain approximate; modern province boundaries are not period-specific research. Original sector layouts remain available for existing saves. No warranty of historical completeness is made.

Source archives, hashes, build code, and detailed provenance are included in the project's data/geography and docs folders.

17 September 2026: the WWII Aden Protectorate gap is supplemented with Natural Earth administrative polygons, clipped to preserve existing historical territories and represented under British protection. Internal divisions remain approximate.

Country flags

Local dated flags use Fredrik Niemelä’s flags collection (metadata CC BY-SA 4.0) and individual Wikimedia Commons sources. Images retain their original licenses. Per-flag source links and additional attribution are included in the flag catalog; data/flags retains licenses and Commons metadata. See docs/FLAGS.md for coverage and historical limits.
